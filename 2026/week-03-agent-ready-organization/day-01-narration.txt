At nine twelve, invoice four forty-seven lands in an agent's queue. Value: eighty-four thousand six hundred dollars. Mismatch: six point two percent. The instruction is simple: resolve material discrepancies with the appropriate approver.

Accounts Payable says, hold it. Procurement says, protect supply. Operations says, pay now, correct it later.

Three reasonable answers. One agent waiting for a rule.

It cannot decide whose priority wins, what material means, or who approves an exception.

Humans bridge ambiguity through experience and informal conversations. The agent makes the invisible operating model visible.

Before asking whether the agent can reason, ask whether the organization can explain the work: outcome, owner, decision right, exception path, and success measure.

The first agent-readiness test is surprisingly human: can two experienced people explain the same decision in the same way?
