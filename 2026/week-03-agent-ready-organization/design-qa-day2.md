Day 1's paper-theatre story and cover establish the series language: a 4:5 stage, warm paper surfaces, deep green and coral accents, character cutouts, large editorial typography, and a compact progress rail.

## Day 2 comparison

- Preserves the recurring stage, palette, editorial typography, AI-agent character, and paper-card treatment.
- Turns the RACI example into a workplace incident rather than a narrated diagram.
