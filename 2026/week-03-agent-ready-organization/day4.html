    The distinction that matters

    Model capability is about recognizing and recommending. Operating readiness is about whether the surrounding organization can make the recommendation safe, timely, and accountable.

    Boundary: this educational drill does not assess model accuracy, security, integration reliability, legal compliance, or production readiness.

    The operating-readiness method

    Five signals before a bounded pilot

    Each signal describes a capability the organization must supply. An agent cannot supply it by sounding confident.

    01

    Owner

    A named decision owner, backup, and authority limit for the actual operating window.

    02

    Evidence

    Source access, freshness, provenance, and uncertainty visible to the person deciding.

    03

    Intervene

    Enough time, authority, and interface support to pause, question, or override.

    04

    Execute

    A controlled action, retained record, and recovery path—not merely an approval click.

    05

    Learn

    Outcome and exceptions feed review, control changes, and the next operating cycle.

    Research notes

    Why readiness is more than model quality

    Governance is operational

    NIST’s AI RMF treats governance as a cross-cutting function: structures, processes, trained people, impact assessment, and ongoing review—not a one-time model gate.

    Explore the NIST AI RMF Core

    Roles and oversight need definition

    The framework calls for clear roles and communication, differentiated human–AI configurations, and defined, assessed human oversight.

    Read the governance outcomes

    Deployment must learn

    NIST also calls for post-deployment monitoring, appeal and override mechanisms, incident response, recovery, change management, and continual improvement.

    Read the manage outcomes

    This page translates those principles into an educational readiness drill. It is not a validated maturity model or certification.

    Do not label a pilot “human overseen” if the human cannot realistically intervene.

    Availability, evidence, authority, execution and learning must exist during the moment that matters—not only in a policy document.
