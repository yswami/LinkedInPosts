The complete micro-drama

“Payment blocked.” Leadership had an answer: “We added a RACI.” Four roles. One clean diagram.

The agent follows the “A.” Procurement replies: “I own the supplier relationship. I don’t own payment release.”

Finance owns the control. Operations sees the supply risk. Accounts Payable can stop the invoice. Everyone has a letter. No one has the decision.

The missing artifact isn’t another role map. It’s a decision contract.

Try this with one real exception. Ask two experienced people: who decides, who acts, within what limit, using what evidence? Compare their answers. Where they disagree, you found hidden organization design. Make it explicit before the agent executes.
