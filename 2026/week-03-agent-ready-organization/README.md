# Week 03 · The Agent-Ready Organization

A five-day investigation into whether organizational clarity is a practical prerequisite for successful AI-agent implementation.

## Working hypothesis

Organizations with clear, measurable, and adaptable definitions of work—roles, decision rights, handoffs, outcomes, and exceptions—have a higher probability of implementing AI agents successfully.

The series treats this as a hypothesis to examine, not a proven causal law. Clear roles are one component of a broader idea: **organizational legibility**.

## Planned arc

1. Diagnose organizational ambiguity before blaming the agent.
2. Separate roles, responsibilities, decision rights, and accountability. **Complete.**
3. Redesign the division of labour between humans and agents. **Complete.**
4. Measure organizational readiness separately from model capability.
5. Build an adaptable organizational interface for human-agent work.
