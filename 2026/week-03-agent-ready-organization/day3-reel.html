The complete micro-drama

By Wednesday, the decision contract was finally clear. Leadership made the obvious leap: “Good. Let the agent handle the rest.”

The agent gathered the evidence, compared the records and recommended an override. Then it moved toward payment release. Finance interrupted: “Stop. Who authorized that?”

The contract defined the decision. It did not define how far the agent could go. Autonomy isn’t a switch. It is an envelope.

For each stage, ask: what is the impact? Can it be reversed? Is the evidence complete? Does policy cover this case? Is it novel?

Here, the agent can gather, analyse, recommend, execute after approval and monitor. The human still owns the high-impact exception. Delegate the stage, not the whole job.
