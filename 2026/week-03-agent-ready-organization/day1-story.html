Accessible transcript

The complete play

At nine twelve Monday, an eighty-four-thousand-six-hundred-dollar invoice enters the agent's queue. Mismatch: six point two percent. Supplier warning: shipments may stop in forty-eight hours.

The rulebook has one sentence: resolve material discrepancies with the appropriate approver. Then the cast arrives. Accounts Payable says hold it; records do not match. Procurement says release it; protect supply. Operations says pay it; correct it later. Three reasonable answers. Together, no decision.

The reveal: the agent is not confused. The organization has never made five things explicit: outcome, owner, decision right, exception path, and success measure. Humans bridge gaps through memory and hallway conversations. Agents cannot safely inherit rules that were never written down.

Before adding an agent, give the same exception to two experienced people. If they cannot explain the decision the same way, you do not have an agent problem. You have an organization-design problem.
