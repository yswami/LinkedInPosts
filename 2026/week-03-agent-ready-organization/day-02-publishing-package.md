## Reader takeaway

A role map names participation. An executable decision contract also defines the trigger, evidence, recommendation, decision, execution, authority boundary, escalation path, and retained record.

## First comment / research note

This post does not argue that RACI is useless. It tests a narrower proposition: participation labels do not automatically specify a machine-executable decision. The framing is grounded in organization-design research on division of labour and integration of effort, and NIST guidance to define and differentiate human–AI roles, oversight, communication, and accountability.

Research links are included in the Day 2 experiment.
