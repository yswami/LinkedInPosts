# Synthetic Personas in 60 Seconds — Storytelling Narration

The narration follows one continuous Project Atlas story. The framework emerges from the decision instead of being presented as a list, while the visuals remain understandable with or without sound. Timestamps align with the seven scenes in `series.html`.

| Start | Scene | Narration |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 00:00.7 | The decision | Imagine this. Project Atlas is twenty-one days late. AI has a recovery plan. |
| 00:07.3 | Character | Before clicking Apply, ask AI to play your user. A prompt creates a character—not customer evidence. |
| 00:14.3 | Constraints | Give it context, constraints, an evidence threshold, and a boundary around who can act. Now it can challenge your hypothesis. |
| 00:23.0 | Project Atlas | It asks: Can I trace the source? If we move these architects, what else moves? Who approves? The simulation earns value by exposing questions your design must answer. |
| 00:34.1 | Evidence | Credibility comes from people: observation, recurring patterns, and an evidence trail. Without those, any archetype is assumed. |
| 00:43.2 | OCEAN | OCEAN adds variation. One explores; another demands proof. It changes the voice—not facts, authority, or evidence. |
| 00:52.1 | Conclusion | That is a synthetic persona: a behavioral opponent for your hypothesis. Let it challenge you, then validate with customers. |

The default render uses the macOS Sandy voice at 205 words per minute. Set `SERIES_VOICE` or `SERIES_RATE` when running `tools/build-series-audio.cjs` to create another version.
