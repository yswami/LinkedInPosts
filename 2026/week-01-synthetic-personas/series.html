The complete story

  1. The decision: Project Atlas is twenty-one days late, and AI proposes a recovery plan. Would you click Apply?
  2. Find an opponent: Before acting, ask AI to play your toughest user—but remember that a job title and prompt create a character, not customer evidence.
  3. Make the character useful: Operating context, behavioral constraints, evidence thresholds and decision boundaries turn it into a hypothesis reviewer.
  4. Hear the challenge: The reviewer asks whether the recommendation is traceable, whether portfolio consequences are visible and who controls the next action.
  5. Trace the behavior: Defensible archetypes begin with real observation, recurring patterns and an evidence record. Unsupported behavior remains an assumption.
  6. Add controlled variation: OCEAN may alter response tendencies, but it does not create facts, authority or evidence.
  7. Use the boundary: Let the simulation challenge the hypothesis, then validate it with real customers.
