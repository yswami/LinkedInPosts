The complete story

At eight-oh-five on Monday, an AI agent reviews Project Meridian. The approved budget is eighty million dollars. Actual cost is forty-six million, and estimate to complete is forty-two million. That puts estimate at completion at eighty-eight million: an eight-million-dollar overrun.

Easy alert? Not yet. Actuals are thirty-six hours old. Nine hundred contractor hours await approval. A six-million-dollar commitment may already be included in the forecast, or it may not. Exchange rates have moved. The same screen can now imply exposure between eight and fifteen point four million dollars.

So what should the agent do? Not declare an overrun. Ask for validation. Show the data timestamp, unresolved transactions, commitment treatment, currency assumptions, and the resulting range. Once those are reconciled, the financial signal can be evaluated against policy.

The first job of a financially governed agent is not to sound certain. It is to know when the evidence is strong enough to interrupt someone, and when uncertainty itself is the alert.
