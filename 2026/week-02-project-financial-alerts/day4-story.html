The complete story

At 8:40, Project Meridian’s overrun is validated, escalated, and explained. The agent proposes moving two shared architects from Project Beacon to Meridian.

On Meridian alone, the proposal looks excellent. EAC falls from eighty-eight to eighty-four million, and the forecast delay shrinks from twenty-one days to eight.

But Beacon now slips eighteen days, and its EAC rises by two-point-five million. A twelve-million-dollar acceptance milestone also moves into the next quarter.

Across the portfolio, the improvement is only one-point-five million. The dashboard promised four. A phased-scope alternative improves EAC by two-point-five million with no Beacon impact, but leaves Meridian four days later.

Now ask which trade-off fits portfolio priority—not which project looks better. Simulate cost, schedule, cash timing, dependencies, and approvals before recommending recovery. Optimize the portfolio, not the loudest project.
