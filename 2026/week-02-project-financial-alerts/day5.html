Generated authority statement

The agent may reconcile evidence, apply the approved materiality policy, explain uncertainty, compare portfolio scenarios, recommend an option, and prepare an approval packet. It may not update forecasts, reassign resources, change milestones, or communicate a committed portfolio decision.

Required controls

    Series boundary: model confidence can support a recommendation. It cannot manufacture data quality, policy, permission, accountability, or approval.

    The complete implementation pattern

    Six gates, one governed decision.

    01

    Validate

    Reconcile source records, timestamps, versions and pending transactions.

    02

    Apply policy

    Use approved materiality, priority, timing and routing rules.

    03

    Explain

    Separate facts, assumptions, scenarios and the next decision.

    04

    Simulate

    Expose cost, schedule, cash, capacity and dependency effects.

    05

    Approve

    Route each consequential action to the authorized decision owner.

    06

    Execute

    Validate again, commit only the approved scope, log and recover.

    Responsible autonomy is not one global switch.

    It is a contract for each action: evidence required, policy applied, authority verified, side effects checked, audit recorded, and recovery available.
