What is still missing

    Day 3 boundary: make uncertainty usable. Recommending recovery actions and testing their cross-project effects is the Day 4 problem.

    Implementation pattern

    Separate financial state from forecast uncertainty.

    01

    Label

    Distinguish posted actuals, commitments, estimate to complete, and assumptions.

    02

    Bound

    Use governed scenarios or sensitivity analysis. If no supported range exists, say so.

    03

    Explain

    Name evidence freshness, confidence drivers, and the conditions that could move the forecast.

    04

    Decide

    Keep the policy classification stable and state the next human decision, owner, and timing.

    A range can create false confidence too.

    The $85M–$92M span is an illustrative planning-scenario range. It is not a probability distribution or statistical confidence interval. Real ranges must come from governed scenario logic, sensitivity analysis, or documented assumptions—not an LLM guess.
