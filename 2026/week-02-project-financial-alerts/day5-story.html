The complete story

A sick day moved Friday's finale to Saturday—fitting for a story about forecasts.

Project Meridian began with a claim: eight million dollars over budget.

Validate the evidence. Apply materiality policy. Explain uncertainty without false precision. Simulate recovery across Beacon, portfolio cost, and cash timing.

Then ask: what may the agent actually do?

It can reconcile records, classify and explain the signal, compare scenarios, and prepare approval.

But changing the forecast, moving shared architects, or shifting a contractual milestone is consequential.

That needs an authorized approver, transaction-level validation, an audit trail, and a recovery path.

The pattern: validate, policy, explain, simulate, approve, then execute.

Model confidence does not create decision authority. Grant autonomy one action at a time.
