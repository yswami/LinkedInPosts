Why

    Day 2 boundary: classify materiality and route the interruption. Explaining uncertainty without false precision is the Day 3 problem.

    Implementation pattern

    Make the policy deterministic and the explanation adaptive.

    01

    Qualify

    Begin with a validated financial signal, not an unresolved calculation.

    02

    Contextualize

    Evaluate residual exposure, persistence, time to act, and consequence.

    03

    Classify

    Use governed rules to assign MONITOR, REVIEW, or ESCALATE.

    04

    Route

    Let AI explain the classification and tailor it to the policy-defined audience.

    The percentage is an input—not the decision.

    The values, score, contingency treatment, and routes in this lab are fictional. Real organizations must define them through financial governance, risk policy, decision rights, and audit requirements.
